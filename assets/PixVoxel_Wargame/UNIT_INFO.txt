UNIT INFO

These are the weapons that each unit has, and the number that is
assigned to each weapon.  Weapon 0 is meant to be usable for
counterattacks, while Weapon 1 may be more powerful but only usable
as part of a chosen action.  This also has an alternate name for
the units, so Infantry_P becomes Bazooka, Plane_S becomes Fighter
Jet and Tank_S becomes AA Tank.  AA is short for Anti-Air.

These are the receive strengths to use when a given unit attacks with a weapon.
Mostly, the explosion size or the amount of sparks flying gets bigger as
strength goes up. But for some, like Rockets, it doesn't change size, but rather
timing -- the Infantry_P's Rocket is timed so that with a strength of 3, it hits
at the right time.  Receive animations use the palette of the attacker, or 0 if
there isn't an animation available in your palette.  They expect the defender to be
facing in the direction of the attack (ready to counterattack, in some games).

These are the depths for the various terrain tiles.
When drawing a unit standing or flying on top of terrain,
 multiply the depth by 3 to get the number of pixels to offset
 the unit's current y position by (higher depth == higher up).

Plains: 1
Forest: 3
Desert: 1
Jungle: 3
Hills: 5
Mountains: 6
Ruins: 4
Tundra: 2
Road: 1
River: 1
Basement (for below buildings): 4
Sea: 1
